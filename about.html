What is RateMe?

RateMe is a social personality-rating platform where you create a profile of traits you care about, share your unique link with friends, and discover how they honestly rate you. View beautiful radar charts, track your top traits, and unlock a personality trivia game once you've collected enough ratings.

How it Works

  1. Create an account with your email and username.
  2. Build your profile by selecting 3–10 traits you want feedback on.
  3. Share your link via WhatsApp, Facebook, X, or anywhere else.
  4. Watch the results come in live with charts and summaries.
  5. Unlock the game at 8 ratings and test your self-awareness!
